How to Play

Master the galaxy — Commander's Briefing

Deep Space Combat is a fast-paced space shooter. Your mission: survive the waves, destroy enemies, and dominate the leaderboard.

🕹️ Controls

  • Arrow Keys / WASD — Move your ship
  • Spacebar — Fire weapon
  • P — Pause / Resume game
  • R — Restart after game over

📱 On mobile: use the on-screen touch controls.

🎯 Objective

Destroy incoming enemy ships and asteroids before they reach you. Each enemy defeated gives you points. Survive as long as possible to climb the leaderboard.

⚡ Power-Ups

  • 🔵 Shield — Temporary invincibility
  • 🔴 Rapid Fire — Increased fire rate
  • 🟡 Score Boost — 2x points for a limited time
  • 💚 Health — Restore one life

❤️ Lives & Game Over

You start with 3 lives. You lose a life when an enemy or asteroid hits your ship. When all lives are lost, the game ends and your final score is recorded.

🏆 Scoring

  • Small enemy: 100 pts
  • Medium enemy: 250 pts
  • Boss enemy: 1000 pts
  • Asteroid: 50 pts

💡 Tips

  • Stay near the center to dodge from any direction
  • Prioritize power-up pickups — they can save your run
  • Boss enemies appear every 5 waves — save your shield for them
  • Watch the edges — enemies can spawn from any side

Good luck, Commander. The galaxy is counting on you. 🚀
